FTSE Russell Names First 10 Nigerian Stocks for Frontier Index Series

business

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

FTSE Russell has confirmed the first 10 Nigerian large-cap stocks eligible for its Frontier Index Series, including GTCO, Zenith Bank, MTN Nigeria and Dangote Cement, ahead of Nigeria's return to Frontier Market status on September 21.

Global index provider FTSE Russell has confirmed the first formal list of Nigerian large-cap stocks eligible for its Frontier Index Series, ahead of Nigeria's return to Frontier Market status effective September 21, 2026.

The 10 large-cap stocks named are Guaranty Trust Holding Company, Zenith Bank, MTN Nigeria Communications, Dangote Cement, Stanbic IBTC Holdings, First HoldCo, Nestle Nigeria, Nigerian Breweries, Presco and Aradel Holdings. FTSE Russell also listed 10 Nigerian stocks in the Mid Cap tier and 11 in the Small Cap tier, bringing the total to 31 Nigerian equities across the index.

The reclassification, confirmed in a market notice on August 27, follows extensive engagement between NGX Group, the Securities and Exchange Commission, FTSE Russell and global custodians to resolve concerns around Nigeria's transition to a T+1 settlement cycle. Nigeria was downgraded from Frontier Market to Unclassified status in September 2023 due to foreign exchange illiquidity, and the reclassification marks a significant milestone in restoring visibility among global frontier-market investors.
