Ex-Minister Sylva Resigns From APC, Calls Party 'Irredeemable'

news

By Staff Reporter · September 2, 2026

Former Minister of State for Petroleum Resources Timipre Sylva has resigned from the APC, calling the party irredeemable, amid allegations linking him to the financing of a failed coup plot which he denies.

Former Minister of State for Petroleum Resources and ex-Bayelsa State Governor Timipre Sylva has resigned from the All Progressives Congress, describing the party as irredeemable and accusing its leaders of embracing the philosophy that all is fair in politics.

In a resignation letter dated August 31, 2026, addressed to the APC Chairman of Ward 4 in Brass Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, Sylva said he took the decision after consulting widely with his family, associates, colleagues and sympathisers. He described himself as a founding member of the APC.

Sylva's resignation comes amid reports linking him to the alleged financing of a failed coup plot, which he has denied. In April 2026, six defendants were arraigned before the Federal High Court in Abuja on charges including treason, terrorism and money laundering in connection with the wider investigation. Sylva is reported to be outside Nigeria. Copies of his resignation letter were sent to the APC National Chairman and the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission.
