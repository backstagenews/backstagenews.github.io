NAICOM Launches Insurance Sector Strengthening Programme to Deepen Coverage

business

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

The National Insurance Commission has launched the Insurance Sector Strengthening Programme, targeting an increase in insurance penetration from 0.5 percent of GDP to 10 percent by 2031.

The National Insurance Commission (NAICOM) has launched the Insurance Sector Strengthening Programme (ISSP), a new initiative aimed at expanding insurance coverage, improving public confidence and positioning the industry as a stronger contributor to Nigeria's economic development.

Commissioner for Insurance, Olusegun Ayo Omosehin, unveiled the programme in Abuja, describing it as a landmark intervention to make Nigeria's insurance sector more inclusive, innovative, resilient and trusted. The five-year, 36-60 month initiative targets an increase in insurance penetration from the current 0.5 percent of GDP to 10 percent by 2031, with only about 11 million Nigerians currently holding insurance policies.

Omosehin identified limited public understanding, a persistent trust deficit, capacity gaps and fragmented distribution channels as key challenges holding back the sector, noting that women represent only 32 percent of policyholders nationwide while youths aged 18-35 account for less than 20 percent of the customer base. He warned that market expansion must go hand in hand with strong regulation, saying, "Innovation will be encouraged, but not at the expense of consumer protection."
