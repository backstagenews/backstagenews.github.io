Atiku Raises Alarm Over Reports of Young Nigerians Selling Kidneys to Survive

news

By Staff Reporter · September 5, 2026

ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar has expressed concern over reports that young Nigerians are selling their kidneys for as little as N1.7 million, blaming the trend on economic hardship under the Tinubu administration.

African Democratic Congress presidential candidate and former Vice President Atiku Abubakar has raised alarm over reports that some young Nigerians are resorting to selling their kidneys to cope with the country's rising cost of living.

In a statement on his X account, Atiku said he had heard disturbing stories of young people selling body organs, particularly kidneys, for as little as N1.7 million in desperate attempts to raise money. He described the reported trend as a consequence of rising costs of food, transport, rent, school fees, medicine and electricity under the Tinubu administration.

"Young Nigerians should be building careers, starting businesses, raising families and chasing their dreams. Not selling their kidneys," Atiku said. He acknowledged that Nigeria has laws prohibiting commercial organ sales and trafficking, but argued that legislation alone would not address the poverty and desperation driving the phenomenon. "This is the human cost of Nigeria's cost-of-living crisis. Tinubu made Nigeria expensive. I will make Nigeria affordable again," he added.
