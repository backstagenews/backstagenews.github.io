Fubara Signs N1.85 Trillion Rivers 2026 Budget Into Law

news

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

Rivers State Governor Siminalayi Fubara has signed the N1.85 trillion 2026 Appropriation Bill into law, the first budget he has assented to as governor since a prolonged political crisis with the state legislature.

Rivers State Governor, Siminalayi Fubara, has signed the N1.85 trillion 2026 Appropriation Bill into law, in what could mark a new phase in his administration's relationship with the state legislature after a prolonged political crisis.

The signing, done at the Government House in Port Harcourt, is the first budget Fubara has assented to as governor since assuming office in 2023. The governor described the development as "a breath of fresh air" and expressed optimism that the passage and signing of the budget, christened the "Budget of Resilience for Growth and Development," would usher in a period of cooperation among the state's political actors.

Funding for the budget will come from Internally Generated Revenue of N487.6 billion, Federation Account Allocation Committee receipts of N936.05 billion, an opening and closing balance of N48 billion, internal loans of N50 billion, and proceeds from asset sales of N25 billion. Lawmakers expunged a N6 billion allocation to Pamo University of Medical Sciences pending an investigation into how the institution used previous funds. Fubara commended the Assembly for its diligence and pledged a cordial working relationship with all arms of government going forward.
