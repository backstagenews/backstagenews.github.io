Police Pay N2.3bn to Families of 2,971 Fallen Officers

news

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

The Nigeria Police Force has disbursed N2.317 billion in insurance and welfare benefits to 2,971 families and next-of-kin of officers who died in the line of duty, covering insurance policy years from 2017 to 2025.

The Nigeria Police Force has disbursed N2.317 billion in insurance and welfare benefits to 2,971 beneficiaries and next-of-kin of deceased police officers, covering insurance policy years spanning 2017 to 2025.

Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Disu, presented the cheques to beneficiaries in Abuja under the Nigeria Police Force Group Life Assurance, Group Personal Accident and IGP Family Welfare Schemes. Disu said the payments were legitimate entitlements arising from the service and sacrifices of officers who died while serving the nation, with some cheques representing outstanding balances recovered from insurance companies after earlier partial settlements.

"Every officer represented here today was a member of a family before becoming a member of the Nigeria Police Force," Disu said. "They were fathers, mothers, husbands, wives, sons, and daughters who carried personal hopes and responsibilities, yet chose to serve a nation that demanded courage and sacrifice from them." The disbursement comes about five months after a similar exercise in which the police released N2.4 billion to 1,075 beneficiaries of officers who died in active service.
