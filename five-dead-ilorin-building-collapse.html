Five Dead as Three-Storey Building Collapses in Ilorin

news

By Staff Reporter · September 2, 2026

A mother and four of her children died after a three-storey building collapsed in the Popo Giwa area of Ilorin, Kwara State, with the surviving father and two children hospitalised.

Five members of the same family, a mother and four of her children, have died after a three-storey building collapsed at Popo Giwa in Ilorin, the Kwara State capital, in the early hours of Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

The building, located at No. 41 Popo Giwa Street beside Gogoro Mosque, collapsed at about 5:00am, trapping eight members of the family beneath the rubble. The Kwara State Fire Service and State Emergency Management Agency said four people were recovered dead initially, with the toll later rising to five following the recovery of an additional victim; four other family members, including the father and two children, were rescued alive and remain hospitalised.

An eyewitness said the father of the family is a serving soldier, while the mother worked as a casual staff member at the Michael Imoudu National Institute for Labour Studies, Ilorin. The deceased were buried according to Islamic rites at the Muslim Cemetery, Oko-Olowo. The family has since vacated the remaining section of the building and agreed to demolish it after it reportedly failed an integrity test, as authorities continue investigations into the cause of the collapse.
