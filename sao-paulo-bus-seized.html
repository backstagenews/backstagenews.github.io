Bus hired to transport São Paulo seized in Bolivia with 86kg of marijuana

Police detained three people after four bags of marijuana were found in a bus contracted to collect São Paulo’s delegation ahead of its Copa Sudamericana match against Bolívar.

Backstage Desk • 12 August 2026

Photo supplied by the publisher.

A bus hired to transport São Paulo’s football delegation in Bolivia was seized after police discovered approximately 86 kilograms of marijuana inside it, according to reports from the country.

The vehicle had been contracted through the transport company Buses Cosmos to collect the Brazilian team as it travelled for its Copa Sudamericana fixture against Bolívar. Authorities reportedly found the drugs in four bags and arrested three people.

The bus was stopped near Cochabamba before São Paulo’s delegation used it. The transport provider arranged another vehicle, allowing the team’s travel arrangements to continue.

São Paulo not implicated

The bus was not owned by São Paulo FC and was not carrying the club’s players or staff at the time of the seizure. The club and its delegation have not been accused of involvement in the alleged drug trafficking case.

São Paulo had arrived in Santa Cruz de la Sierra before continuing its journey toward La Paz for the match. Reports said the logistical issue was resolved by the contracted transport company.

What is known

  • About 86kg of marijuana was found in the vehicle.
  • The bus was hired to transport São Paulo’s delegation; it was not the club’s own bus.
  • Police reportedly arrested three people.
  • A replacement bus was provided, and São Paulo’s travel continued.

Reporting source: AS: “Encuentran 86 kilos de marihuana en el autobús que debía trasladar a São Paulo”.
