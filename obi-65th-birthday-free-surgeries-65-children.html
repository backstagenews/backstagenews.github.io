Obi Marks 65th Birthday With 65 Free Surgeries for Children

news

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi has marked his 65th birthday by sponsoring 65 free paediatric surgeries at Mother Mary Hospital in Isolo, Lagos, saying the initiative reflects his belief that birthdays are opportunities to give back.

Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi has marked his 65th birthday with the completion of 65 free paediatric surgeries at Mother Mary Hospital, Isolo, Lagos.

The intervention, carried out by Dr Folaseye Adebayo and her medical team, provided surgical care for children suffering from breathing difficulties, recurrent infections and sleep-related complications. The surgeries included adenotonsillectomy, the removal of enlarged or chronically infected tonsils and adenoids, a specialist procedure many low-income families cannot afford.

Obi disclosed the completion of the surgeries at the final follow-up clinic and appreciation programme at the hospital, where parents shared testimonies of significant improvements in their children's health. "For me, birthdays are not merely occasions for celebration. They are opportunities to give back, touch lives, and make a meaningful difference in the lives of others," Obi said, adding that all 65 children had successful outcomes.
