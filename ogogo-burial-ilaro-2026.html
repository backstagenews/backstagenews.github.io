Ogogo Laid to Rest in Ilaro as Yinka Quadri, Nollywood Colleagues Bid Final Farewell

entertainment

By Staff Reporter · August 28, 2026 · 1 min read

Veteran actor Taiwo Hassan, popularly known as Ogogo, was laid to rest in his hometown of Ilaro, Ogun State, with Yinka Quadri and other Nollywood colleagues among mourners at the funeral rites.

Veteran Yoruba Nollywood actor Taiwo Hassan, popularly known as Ogogo, was laid to rest in his hometown of Ilaro, Ogun State, as colleagues, family and fans gathered for his funeral rites.

Veteran actor Yinka Quadri, a close friend of the late actor, was among the Nollywood figures present at the burial, offering words of comfort to the family. Ogogo died on August 23, 2026, at the age of 66 after a battle with stage-four cancer, and his family had earlier announced a Fidau prayer for August 31 as part of Islamic funeral rites.

Tributes have continued to pour in from across the Nigerian film industry for the veteran actor, whose career in Yoruba cinema spanned more than four decades.
