INEC to Release Final List of 2027 Presidential and NASS Candidates on September 12

news

By Staff Reporter · September 5, 2026

The Independent National Electoral Commission is set to publish the final list of candidates for the January 16, 2027 presidential and National Assembly elections on September 12, with 19 presidential candidates confirmed.

The Independent National Electoral Commission is set to publish the final list of candidates for the January 16, 2027 presidential and National Assembly elections on Saturday, September 12, 2026, in compliance with Section 32(1) of the Electoral Act 2026.

Officials of the commission are currently fine-tuning the list ahead of its publication. The APC, PDP and ADC are expected to present candidates for all 469 National Assembly seats, comprising 109 in the Senate and 360 in the House of Representatives. The Nigeria Democratic Congress will present candidates for 109 Senatorial seats and 219 House of Representatives seats.

INEC had last month released the provisional list of 19 presidential candidates, including their running mates and other particulars. However, some aspirants are currently challenging their substitution by their parties in court, including Senator Gabriel Suswam of the APC, who is contesting the party's decision to replace him as its Benue North East Senatorial District candidate after he won the primary election.
