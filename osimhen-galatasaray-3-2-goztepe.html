Osimhen Scores and Assists as Galatasaray Beat Goztepe 3-2

sports

By Staff Reporter · August 29, 2026

Victor Osimhen scored and provided an assist as Galatasaray came from behind to beat Goztepe 3-2 in the Turkish Super Lig.

Victor Osimhen scored and set up another goal as Galatasaray came from behind to beat Goztepe 3-2 in the Turkish Super Lig on Saturday, August 29, 2026.

Osimhen continues to be central to Galatasaray's attack this season, contributing directly to goals in back-to-back matches as the club pushes for another strong domestic campaign. The comeback win showcased the team's resilience after falling behind early in the match.

Galatasaray's victory keeps them firmly in contention near the top of the Super Lig table as the season progresses. Osimhen's continued goal involvement will be a boost heading into the club's upcoming European fixtures.
