Bus hired to transport São Paulo seized in Bolivia with 86kg of marijuana

Police detained three people after finding four bags containing marijuana in a bus contracted to collect São Paulo’s delegation ahead of its match against Bolívar.

Backstage Desk • 12 August 2026

The bus was supplied by a subcontracted transport company and was due to carry the Brazilian club’s delegation in Bolivia. Reports say the vehicle was seized near Cochabamba before the team used it; the company arranged a replacement, and São Paulo’s travel plans continued. There is no indication that São Paulo or its players were involved.
