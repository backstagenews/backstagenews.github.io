IGP Disu Hosts Chief of Air Staff, Pledges Stronger Police-Air Force Joint Operations

news

By Staff Reporter · September 2, 2026

Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Disu hosted the Chief of the Air Staff at the Force Headquarters, pledging deeper collaboration between the Nigeria Police Force and Nigerian Air Force to tackle evolving security threats.

The Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Rilwan Disu, received the Chief of the Air Staff, Air Marshal Sunday Kelvin Aneke, at the Force Headquarters in Abuja, in a move aimed at deepening inter-agency collaboration to tackle Nigeria's evolving security threats.

Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Ani Iniedu, said the visit focused on strengthening the longstanding partnership between the Nigeria Police Force and the Nigerian Air Force, with emphasis on joint operations, intelligence sharing and enhanced air support for police tactical missions.

Both service chiefs reaffirmed their commitment to inter-agency synergy as part of ongoing efforts to combat banditry, terrorism and other forms of organised crime across the country. The meeting is expected to pave the way for more coordinated air and ground operations in high-risk areas.
