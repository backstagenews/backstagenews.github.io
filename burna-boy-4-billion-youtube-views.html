Burna Boy Makes History as First Nigerian Artist to Cross 4 Billion YouTube Views

entertainment

By Staff Reporter · September 3, 2026

Afrobeats star Burna Boy has become the first Nigerian and Sub-Saharan African artist to surpass 4 billion total YouTube views, driven by hits including Last Last, Ye and On The Low.

Nigerian Afrobeats star Burna Boy has surpassed four billion total views on YouTube, becoming the first Nigerian and Sub-Saharan African artist to reach the milestone.

The figure combines views across videos on his official YouTube channel, including hit songs such as "On The Low," "Ye," "Last Last" and "For My Hand." The achievement builds on Burna Boy's previous YouTube record, when he became the first Nigerian artist to cross one billion total views on the platform back in 2021.

The Grammy-winning singer's numbers have continued climbing steadily since then, aided by acclaimed albums including African Giant and Twice As Tall, alongside a globally successful touring career; his I Told Them... tour reportedly grossed more than $30 million. The milestone underscores the continued global growth of Afrobeats and Burna Boy's standing as one of the genre's most decorated crossover acts.
