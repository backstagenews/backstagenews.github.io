US Strikes Deal for Majority Control of 65 Billion Barrels of Venezuelan Oil

news

By Staff Reporter · September 1, 2026

President Trump has announced a deal giving the United States majority control of more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuela's oil reserves, which he described as the biggest oil deal in world history.

US President Donald Trump has announced a deal giving the United States majority control of more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuela's proven oil reserves, describing it as "the biggest oil deal in world history."

Trump said Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, working with Venezuela's interim President Delcy Rodriguez, secured the agreement through a partnership with private business, at no cost to American taxpayers. The deal, he said, more than doubles US oil reserves at a time when the Strategic Petroleum Reserve has fallen to its lowest levels since the 1980s.

Rodriguez said the 25-year deal preserves Venezuela's "ownership and sovereignty" over its resources while allowing development of 17 strategic oilfields with an initial production target of 1.5 million barrels per day. She said Venezuela could see more than $200 billion in additional revenue over the life of the deal, with $19 from every barrel sold to the US flowing to Caracas. The agreement follows a US military operation in Venezuela in January 2026 that led to the capture of former president Nicolas Maduro, who faces narco-terrorism charges in a New York federal court.
