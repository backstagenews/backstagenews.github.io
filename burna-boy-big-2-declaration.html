Burna Boy: "There's No Big 3 — Just the Big 2, and Then Me"

entertainment

By Staff Reporter · August 11, 2026 · 1 min read

Burna Boy performing live. Photo: “Burna Boy (cropped)” via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Burna Boy has stirred fresh conversation online after distancing himself from the long-running "Big 3" tag shared with Davido and Wizkid.

Burna Boy has stirred fresh conversation across Nigerian social media after distancing himself from the long-running "Big 3" tag that groups him with Davido and Wizkid as the country's biggest music stars.

Speaking during a live performance, the Grammy-winning singer told the crowd there was no such thing as a "Big 3" — only a "Big 2," made up of Davido and Wizkid, with Burna Boy standing apart from both. "Next time anybody tells you about the 'Big 3,' tell them there's only the 'Big 2' — and then there's Burna Boy," he said, drawing loud cheers from the crowd.

The clip has circulated widely online, reigniting a debate that has followed Nigerian Afrobeats fandoms for years: who really sits at the top of the industry. Supporters of Wizkid and Davido pushed back online, while Burna Boy's fanbase — known as the Outsiders — embraced the line as further proof of his self-assurance.

It isn't the first time Burna Boy has positioned himself outside the "Big 3" framing; he's made similar claims before about his standing relative to his two most talked-about peers.

Whether the comment settles anything is unlikely — if anything, it's added fresh fuel to one of Afrobeats' most enduring arguments.

The "Big 3" label has followed Nigerian Afrobeats for years, used informally by fans and commentators to describe Wizkid, Davido, and Burna Boy as the genre's most commercially dominant, internationally recognised acts — each having headlined major arenas and stadiums across the UK, US, and Europe.

Comparisons between the three resurface almost every time one of them hits a new commercial milestone, whether a chart position, an award nomination, or a streaming record, with rival fanbases routinely citing the numbers as proof of supremacy. Burna Boy's comment is likely to feed that same cycle, rather than settle it.
