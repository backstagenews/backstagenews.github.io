Anthony Joshua's Driver Attempted Overtake Before Fatal Crash, Trial Hears

news

By Staff Reporter · September 4, 2026

A court in Nigeria has heard that the driver of a car carrying Anthony Joshua was attempting to overtake a moving truck when it crashed, killing two of the boxer's friends, as the trial was adjourned until September 10.

The driver of a car carrying former world heavyweight boxing champion Anthony Joshua was attempting to overtake another vehicle when it crashed and killed two of the boxer's friends, a court in Ogun State has heard.

Adeniyi Mobolaji Kayode, 46, faces charges including causing death by dangerous driving and driving without a valid national driver's licence, which he denies. The court was told that Joshua's group, who were travelling to visit the boxer's father in Ogun State last December, were asleep when Kayode lost control of his Lexus SUV while overtaking a moving truck and collided with a parked lorry. Joshua's personal trainer, Latif Ayodele, and strength coach, Sina Ghami, died in the crash.

The court also heard that police have yet to take a statement from Joshua himself; under cross-examination, the investigating officer said "efforts are ongoing" but that he had not contacted the boxer because he "didn't have his number." A second defendant, lorry driver Abubakar Adamu, 35, denies a charge of obstructing the free flow of traffic. The trial was adjourned until September 10, more than eight months after the crash. Following the collision, Joshua posted a tribute to his fallen friends, writing that he did not realise "how special they are" until after their deaths.
